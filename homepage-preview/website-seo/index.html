Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the site

Why Squarespace instead of WordPress or a custom build?

Squarespace gives the best balance of design flexibility, speed and maintainability for a portfolio-led business. It handles hosting, security and SSL out of the box, so there are no plugin updates or server maintenance to manage. With custom code blocks it reaches the same visual quality as a fully custom site at a fraction of the ongoing cost.

How long does a full website build take?

Most builds take 4 to 8 weeks from kickoff to launch. The timeline depends on the number of pages, how quickly content and feedback come back, and whether photography is being delivered alongside the build. I set a milestone at each stage.

Do you do SEO?

The site is structured so it can be found: schema, meta, sitemap, location pages where they are useful. That is part of the build, not a separate product.

Is a growth retainer required after launch?

No. The site stands on its own after launch. A monthly plan keeps new project pages and posts moving so it does not go stale.

Can this be bundled with photography or social media?

Yes. The site can run inside a Creative Partner agreement, which covers photography, film, progression, website and social under one monthly conversation.
