Portfolio

Introduction

Recent work across British Columbia, grouped by the landscape that shapes it. Coastal projects on the Sunshine Coast and Howe Sound, alpine builds in Whistler and Pemberton, lakefront homes in the Columbia Valley, and a handful of interiors.

Each project is shown the way I present it in person: design intent first, materials second, the light that made the frame third.

Matt

Oles Cove, Halfmoon Bay

A Black Box on the Bluff

Corrugated black cladding lifted on posts above the slope, white inside and open to the west.

I don't photograph buildings. I photograph the decisions that shaped them: why this cladding, why that angle on the slope, why the window meets the horizon where it does. The architect spent months getting it right. The photography should make that obvious in a single frame.
