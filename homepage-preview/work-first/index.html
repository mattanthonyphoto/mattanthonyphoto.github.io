I am Matt Anthony. I photograph architecture and interiors across British Columbia.

I work from Squamish, for architects, custom builders and interior designers across the province. The work is documenting design intent: the light a room was drawn for, the way one material meets another, the reason a building sits where it does. I am a Transport Canada certified advanced drone pilot.

“What first drew us to Matt's work was his use of natural light. He captures spaces in a way that feels authentic and honest, allowing the materials to shine. Matt truly understands what it takes to showcase a project.”
Jordan Maddox, Sitelines Architecture
