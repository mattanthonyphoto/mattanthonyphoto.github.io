Working Together

I'm based in Squamish, British Columbia and work across the Sea to Sky Corridor: Squamish, Whistler and Pemberton. I also shoot on the Sunshine Coast, in Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Okanagan. I travel for the right project.

Most of my clients are architects, custom home builders, interior designers, and construction companies across British Columbia. I also work with real estate developers, landscape architects, and design build firms who need professional imagery for their portfolios, websites, award submissions, and marketing.

The Process

A typical session includes a pre shoot consultation, a half or full day on site covering exterior, interior, detail and aerial photography, professional editing, and delivery of high resolution images through an online gallery within 5 to 10 business days.

Before every shoot, I review architectural plans, renderings, or previous photos to understand orientation, light conditions, and composition opportunities. I'll send you a prep checklist covering staging tips, timing, and key design details to highlight.

Most residential architectural shoots take between 3 and 6 hours depending on the size of the property and the scope of coverage. Larger properties, multi building sites, or projects that include twilight photography may require a full day or a return visit.

Drone & Technical

Yes. I'm a Transport Canada certified Advanced Drone Pilot. Aerial photography is part of how I work and is included in most architectural shoots. It adds context, reveals a project's relationship to its surroundings, and gives clients a perspective that ground level photography can't achieve on its own.

Yes. Drone flights are regulated by Transport Canada and certain areas have airspace restrictions, including near airports, helipads, and some national parks. I handle all flight planning and regulatory compliance. If a location has restrictions, I'll let you know during the planning stage and suggest alternative approaches if needed.

Yes. I shoot and cut video alongside the stills: ground level walkthroughs, aerial flyovers, and detail sequences. It is finished for web, social media, and presentation use.

I shoot with professional mirrorless cameras and a range of architectural lenses including tilt shift glass for perspective correction. For aerial work, I use a commercial grade drone platform. All post production is done in Lightroom and Photoshop with a focus on natural, editorial quality results.

Deliverables & Licensing

Final images are typically delivered within 5 to 10 business days through an online gallery. You'll receive both high resolution files for print and web optimized versions. Rush delivery is available if you're working toward a deadline.

You'll receive high resolution JPEGs optimized for both print and digital use. Web sized versions and social media crops are available on request. If you need TIFF files or specific formats for a publication, I can accommodate that as well.

Yes. All images come with a commercial usage license that covers your website, social media, print marketing, award submissions, and editorial use. I go over the intended use at booking so the license fits.

Yes. I prepare imagery for award submissions and publication, formatted and composed to the specs those programmes set. The CHBA Awards and most design magazines each ask for something different, and I build to whichever one applies.

Pricing & Packages

Pricing is based on the scope of each project including the number of images, time on site, location, and any additional services like drone photography or videography. I provide custom quotes after an initial conversation about your project.

Yes. For builders, architects, and firms with multiple projects throughout the year, I offer retainer partnerships that include a set number of shoots at a preferred rate. This ensures consistent visual quality across your portfolio and gives you priority scheduling.

A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the balance due upon delivery of the final images. I accept e transfer, credit card, and bank transfer.

Aerial photography is included in most architectural packages. For standalone drone sessions or projects that require extended aerial coverage, it's quoted separately. I'll always be upfront about costs during the proposal stage.
