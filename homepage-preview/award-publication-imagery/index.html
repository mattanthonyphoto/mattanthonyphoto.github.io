Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about awards and publications

We just use our phones for project photos.

For progress shots, phones are great. But awards judges and publication editors only see your work through photography. iPhone images on a Georgie submission compete against professional editorial shots. Your craftsmanship deserves documentation at the same standard it was built.

We can't afford professional photography right now.

With cost-sharing, your out-of-pocket drops to $1,200-$2,500. That is less than 0.2% of a typical project value and generates images you will use across your website, proposals, awards, and publications for 3-5 years.

We already have a photographer.

That is great. Many clients keep their existing photographer for certain projects and bring me in for the signature builds, the award contenders, the ones worth submitting to publications. It does not need to be all or nothing.

We've never submitted to awards before. Is it worth starting?

One submission changes the trajectory. You go from zero credentials to "Award-nominated" or "Award-winning" on every piece of marketing. The first entry is the hardest, and I handle the entire process so you can test the waters without investing your own time.

How long does the submission process take?

For you, almost no time. I need your approval, a credits list, and floor plans from the architect. From there, I handle the photography, descriptions, pitches, and submissions. Most projects go from shoot to submission in 2-3 weeks.

The best projects don't just get built. They get recognized. Awards validate your craft. Publications amplify your reach. One submission changes everything.
