The record

The record of how it went up.

A finished building is one story. The visits before that are another. I come back across the build: framing, envelope, the crew at work, the site as it changes.

Those photographs keep the client in the picture while the project is still going up. They also become the library the firm draws from before the final shoot.

What's Included

What the visits cover

Construction Progression

Framing, formwork, mechanical, envelope. The milestones that show the scale and complexity of your builds, documented at every critical stage.

The crew at work

The people on site. The crane lift. The huddle. A record of who built it, not only what was built.

Portraits on site

Environmental portraits of the crew, shot in context. For the site, proposals, and the posts that follow.

Film when it is useful

Short sequences from the visit when motion helps the record. Not a separate video production.

Drone & Aerial Documentation

Transport Canada certified aerial photography and video. Site overviews, progress documentation and angles a ground-level camera cannot reach.

A library the firm can use

Photographs from each visit, ready for the client update, the site, and the posts that keep the build in view.

Who this service is built for

Builders with Active Sites

You have projects in progress and need content that shows the quality of your work before the drywall goes up.

Firms that want the crew in the record

The people on site are part of how the project went up. Those photographs belong in the library too.

Milestone Documentation

Major pours, beam sets, topping out. The moments that deserve more than a phone photo for your project records and client updates.

Trades and Fabricators

Millwork, glazing, structural steel, concrete. Your craft deserves to be seen in context, not isolated on a white background.

Every day on your job site is content. The crane lift at sunrise. The crew in the rain. The framing against the mountain. Almost none of it gets photographed.
