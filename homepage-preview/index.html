Clarity, accuracy, and intention over spectacle. I photograph the decisions that hold a building together, so the design is still legible in the picture.

Those decisions are where the building sits, why a room opens as it does, which material was chosen, and which view has to read.

I plan around the light to bring each space out at its clearest. The light moves through the building, and each room has a short window, so I photograph it then.

The visit

How a project moves from the first note to the gallery.

Five phases. The same person from the intake to the gallery.

  1. 01

    Project intake

    The story of the project, the rooms that matter, and how the site is reached. Drawings come with it. A call is welcome. It is not required.

  2. 02

    Planning

    I read the drawings, work out the light and the access, and send a note ahead of the day.

  3. 03

    The day

    Exteriors and interiors, aerial where the site allows, and film when it is part of the work.

  4. 04

    Editing

    I edit every frame myself, in Lightroom and Photoshop. Restrained, so the room reads the way it reads standing in it.

  5. 05

    Gallery

    The photographs are in an online gallery, typically within 5 to 10 business days. Licensing is written for the agreed use.
