The three earlier directions

You said the current page still isn't landing, and that all four parts are off: the hero, everything below the work, the length, and the selection. So none of these is a patch on the old page. Each one is a different answer to the same question.

All three use your palette, your typefaces, your nav and your footer. The photographs are your live ones. Nothing here invents a claim. What changes between them is the architecture.

01

Work First

There is no hero. The page opens straight into the work: eight photographs, each filling most of a screen, each carrying only its name and place. Scrolling the homepage is walking through the portfolio. After the last one, a single paragraph, one quote, and the footer. No services, no film strip, no calls to action beyond the button in the nav.

The most subtraction. Closest to GD Nielsen and LRD. Does the work carry the whole page on its own?

02

Masthead

Opens with type instead of a photograph. Your name set large, a hairline rule, one line saying what you do and where, the way a title page reads. Then one photograph set deliberately inside the margins rather than bleeding off the edges, so it reads as a plate. Then the work two up, one quote, and a close.

The direct answer to the 82 percent bounce: a visitor who was referred to you sees your name before any photograph loads. Is that reassurance worth opening on words?

03

Film First

Opens on motion: an eight second silent loop I cut from the Johnstone Road film, with your name over it. Then the work as stills, then all three finished films presented properly. If the video will not load, or the visitor has reduced motion turned on, the first frame shows as a still and nothing breaks.

Film is the thing your competitors do not put on a homepage. Does leading with it say photographer, or does it bury the stills?

Worth knowing before you look

  • None of them is much shorter than the current page. You said it is too long and that is a fair hit on all three. But the count that matters moved a long way: the current page makes you scroll past five sections that are not photographs, and these make you scroll past one, two and three. Say the word on whichever you pick and I will cut it further.
  • Masthead shows your wordmark twice in the first screen, once in the nav and once as the masthead. It reads slightly doubled. Easy fix once it is the chosen one.
  • The film loop restarts on a hard cut back to daylight. Fixable with a crossfade at the loop point if you go that way.
  • These are previews, not the live site. Nothing here has touched mattanthonyphoto.com.
