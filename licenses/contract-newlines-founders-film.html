1.Definitions

In this Agreement:

  • Photographer means Matt Anthony, carrying on business as Matt Anthony Photography.
  • Client means Newlines, operating Newlines and NewlinesADU, acting through Gabriel Viel Cote.
  • Project means the owners interview described in Section 2.
  • Work means the film and the short cuts delivered under this Agreement.
  • Parties means the Photographer and the Client.
2.Scope of services

The Photographer will produce one owners interview for the Client, as follows:

  • One couch setup at Thomas Chamberland's house, on the same Pemberton day as the separate lineup film. About an hour of talking once the microphones are up.
  • The film opens on the work, then Gabriel Viel Cote and Thomas Chamberland, the view behind them.
  • No second interview, and no installation.
  • Sauna and Chestnut footage the Photographer already holds may be cut in. Renovation files are cut in when the Client sends them before the shoot.
  • The Photographer writes the brief. The Client and Thomas Chamberland mark it up once. The script is final in writing before the shoot.
  • Shoot window: the last week of October 2026. The exact day is confirmed by email once the script is final and the footage is in.
  • House: in or near Pemberton, or back toward Whistler if the drone for the lineup film happens before this setup. A longer second leg is not included.

Pemberton travel is included in the lineup film agreement. No separate travel line is billed here. This fee is only the owners interview.

3.Deliverables

After the shoot, and after the balance in Section 5 is paid, the Photographer will deliver:

  • One horizontal film, about 60 to 90 seconds.
  • The same film as a vertical video, for ads.
  • Files through a Dropbox shared folder.

Further vertical ad variations, 15 to 30 seconds with a different opening, are $250 each before GST. They are not included in Section 5. They are billed when the Client asks for them in writing. Media buying is not part of this fee. If the Client wants help with that process, the Parties will agree it separately.

4.Turnaround and revisions
  • Final delivery: about three weeks after the shoot date.
  • Revisions: one round of notes is included. Further revisions are billed at $75 per hour.

7.Licence and copyright

Licence. On full payment, the Photographer grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use the Work on the Client's website and in the Client's own ads and social posts, including short cuts made for that use.

Any other use, including BC Passive House, a trade, a publisher, or another collaborator, comes back through the Photographer.

The Photographer retains copyright in the Work, the right to use the Work in the Photographer's portfolio, website, social media, and marketing, and the right to license the Work to other parties on a project (architect, designer, trades) at the Photographer's discretion. The homeowner is not a licensing party.

Footage the Client supplies, including realtor video, stays owned by the person who shot it. This Agreement does not transfer that copyright. Realtor footage is used only when the Client has written permission from that owner, delivered before the shoot.

Credit. When the Client publishes the Work in editorial, press, or award contexts, the Client will credit "Matt Anthony Photography" wherever practicable. Social posts should tag @mattanthonyphoto when the format permits.

8.Reschedule and weather
  • Either Party may reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice if weather, site readiness, or a scheduling conflict makes the date unworkable, at no additional cost.
  • If the shoot is rescheduled on less than 48 hours' notice, a $250 site mobilization fee may apply where the Photographer has already mobilized or turned down other work to hold the date.
  • The Parties will agree a new date within 21 days of any reschedule.
  • Two reschedules are included at no charge. Further reschedules may incur a $250 site mobilization fee.
9.Cancellation
  • By the Client more than 14 days before the confirmed shoot date: deposit refunded less a $250 administrative fee.
  • By the Client 7 to 14 days before the confirmed shoot date: deposit forfeited.
  • By the Client within 7 days of the confirmed shoot date: the full fee is owed, and the balance becomes immediately due.
  • By the Photographer: deposit returned in full, and reasonable effort made to refer the Client to a comparable photographer.

Cancelling this film does not cancel the lineup film, and the shared day may still go ahead for that film.

10.Access, script, and footage

The Client will:

  • Confirm permission to film at Thomas Chamberland's house, and share the address, parking, and access at least 48 hours before the shoot
  • Finish the script in writing before the shoot, and send any renovation files to be cut in
  • Deliver written permission before the shoot for any realtor footage, or leave that footage out
  • Have Gabriel Viel Cote and Thomas Chamberland available for the one couch setup

The Photographer is not liable for delays or a thinner film caused by site access, missing footage, or a script that is not final.
